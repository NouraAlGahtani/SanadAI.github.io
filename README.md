## ✨ Meet SANAD

**SANAD AI (سند)** explores what incident response could look like when AI investigation meets independent safety controls. The project combines agent-assisted incident analysis, advisory forecasting, adaptive feedback, and safety-gated recovery in a local demonstration environment.

This repository contains the **promotional website**, not the Flask/Ollama application itself.

## 🪄 What you'll discover

| Experience | What it shows |
|---|---|
| 🔎 Incident investigation | How SANAD reasons through simulated operational signals |
| 🔮 Predictive intelligence | Heuristic forecasts and feedback-based calibration |
| 🛡️ Safety-gated recovery | Why AI proposals must pass independent checks |
| 🧠 Adaptive incident memory | How prior observations can inform future suggestions |
| 📊 Transparent evidence | Reported synthetic evaluation results and their limits |

## 🔬 Evidence, with context

Earlier development evaluations reported **20/20 held-out** and **12/12 adversarial** end-to-end scenario passes with Qwen enabled. These are synthetic development results, **not** a fresh validation of the final restored build, production performance, or proof of unseen-family generalization. Qwen safety proposals aligned with expected actions in **5/12** cases; an independent safety controller governed execution. The deterministic fallback also passed the 12 adversarial cases.

The site's incident previews are **illustrative interactions**, not live backend execution. Forecasting is heuristic/adaptive, not a trained neural model.
